# Portfolio demo expansion — agreed direction

Keep the current cinematic design and original travel-estimator foundation. Do not pivot again or claim market uniqueness. Build a broad, coherent portfolio demonstration inspired by discovery, flexible-date comparison, itinerary and expense workflows, plus budget transparency and honeymoon trade-offs.

## Implement now
1. Budget-first trip discovery with explicit sample cost limits, shortfalls and alternatives.
2. Destination experiences, dining suggestions and saved itinerary stops; source provenance and unknown fees visible.
3. Flight scenario comparison, flexible-date sample grid, baggage/transfer additions, watchlists and external provider searches.
4. Day-by-day itinerary editor, checklists, notes, usable-time comparisons and partner preferences.
5. Actual-expense ledger with multiple participants, fair splits, settlements and remaining budget after commitments/reserve.
6. Private shared-trip invitations with explicit recipient confirmation by the user, revocable tokens and role-limited collaboration.
7. Offer transparency: advertised/base prices, taxes, mandatory/optional costs, included perks, assumptions, missing charges and source timestamps.
8. Optional open-data adapters: exchange-rate reference data, near-term weather and reusable Wikivoyage guides. Cache within documented source limits and preserve attribution/licences.
9. FlightAPI adapters and setup instructions; no credentials available yet. Account quota and retrieved quote timestamps must be visible; seller confirmation is still required.
10. Data-source registry, ingestion timestamps, source links, failure/stale states and a reproducible collection script.

## Explicit boundaries
No claim to collect every offer or clone complete commercial platforms. No scraping of sites that prohibit it, bypass of anti-bot controls, copied private reviews, invented real discounts, booking confirmations or payments. Provider errors remain visible. Weather outside the forecast horizon stays unavailable. No ML model will be described as trained until a relevant licensed dataset and held-out evaluation exist. Existing sample data is not real training evidence.

## Later / conditional
Live flight/hotel inventories require external credentials and coverage checks. Continuous deal monitoring requires a deployed scheduler; a local demo will refresh on user request. ML training remains a separate evaluated milestone. Public publishing remains deferred until portfolio completion.
